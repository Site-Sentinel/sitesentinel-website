# Review before handing over copy

- The requested meaning and approved facts are preserved.
- Claims about features, customers and results have a source. Missing evidence
  is identified rather than invented.
- The writing sounds specific to Site Sentinel and its audience.
- Empty introductions, repeated conclusions and forced contrasts are removed.
- Necessary technical details and useful uncertainty remain.
- Australian spelling is used. There are no em dashes or en dashes.
- Website wording is in validated content files, not component markup.
- Changes stay within the requested scope. Existing copy is not rewritten merely
  to satisfy a style preference.

This is an editorial review, not an AI-authorship detector or a machine score.
