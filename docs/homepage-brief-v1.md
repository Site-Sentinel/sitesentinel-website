We secure the physical entrance. > We manage the people. > We manage it in the cloud. >
We connect your existing systems. > We automate access decisions. > We can deploy almost
anywhere. > We deliver the project outcome. > EXPECTATIONS. DELIVERED.

**ALCOHOL BREATH TESTING.**
**INTEGRATED WITH SITE ACCESS.**

This is what demonstrates Site Sentinel orchestrates site-entry rules rather than being a card reader.

### Which mockup to build from

Use **mockup-04** as the visual target: it is the tightest render, has the correct hero
(real container, worker walking through, chainlink fence, blue sky) and shows the genuine
product UI rather than mock dashboards.

Its shortcoming is length. It renders only sections 01-05, 07, 11 and 12, dropping Intelligent
Access (06), breath testing (08), solar (09) and outcomes (10). Take those four sections'
layout from **mockup-02**, which is the only render carrying all twelve.

mockup-03 duplicates the integrations block). Copy comes from this brief, not from the pixels.

## Design tokens, read off the capability statement and mockups

Sample the PDF directly before committing these. Approximate values:

| Token               | Value                         | Use                                                                      |
| ------------------- | ----------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Navy (primary dark) | approx `#0B2A4A` to `#12263F` | Hero overlay, platform section, outcomes section, footer                 |
| Cyan (accent)       | approx `#29B8E5`              | Headline second line ("SAFER SITES.", "ANYWHERE."), icon circles, arrows |
| Blue (mid)          | approx `#1B72B8`              | Secondary buttons, icon fills, links                                     |
| Magenta (CTA)       | approx `#E5177F`              | Primary buttons only. Used sparingly.                                    |
| Green (accent)      | approx `#5BB85B`              | Sustainability ticks and the "Sustainable Solutions" numeral             |
| Off-white / panel   | approx `#F2F6FA`              | Alternating section backgrounds, card panels                             |
| White               | `#FFFFFF`                     | Cards on tinted panels                                                   |

Typography: a humanist sans throughout. Headings are bold uppercase with wide tracking on the
small eyebrows and tight tracking on the large display lines. The capability statement pairs a
heavier weight for the first headline line with the cyan-coloured second line. Body copy is
regular weight, generous line height, dark grey rather than pure black.

The logo is a layered hexagonal "S" in a cyan/blue/magenta gradient, locked up beside
"SITE SENTINEL" over "ACCESS & CONTROL SYSTEMS".

Recurring device: a diagonal cyan-to-magenta slash in the bottom-right of navy sections,
visible in the hero and the final CTA. Worth building as a reusable component.

## Overall visual direction

Do not make this look like a typical security-company website. The positioning is
construction technology / industrial SaaS / engineered solution provider.

- Deep Site Sentinel navy anchors the design. Most content sections get generous white space.
- Cyan, blue and magenta from the logo are controlled accents, not decorative gradients everywhere.
- Large photography shows actual solutions in real project environments. Software screens
  establish the technology side.
- Not wall-to-wall copy. On desktop several sections occupy roughly one screen each, with
  animation and transitions carrying the story.
